📚 Peer-Reviewed Scientific Literature & Global Methodologies

Direct empirical application of international and national peer-reviewed research papers into our multi-agent AI architecture:

BRRI & IWMI (2024)

1. Groundwater Table Depletion in Barind & Northern Basins

Research Finding: Joint BRRI & IWMI research confirms groundwater tables in Barind & Teesta basins deplete by 0.22–0.45m annually, driven primarily by intensive dry-season Boro rice irrigation.

AI Engine Application: Recommends low-water oilseed and pulse rotations (Mustard, Lentils) saving up to 60% irrigation water over monoculture Boro.

NASA GSFC & BWDB (2023)

2. Satellite Precipitation & Teesta Basin Flood Dynamics

Research Finding: NASA GSFC GPM IMERG and BWDB telemetry establish 91.3% forecast accuracy for 30-minute extreme rainfall in sub-Himalayan floodplains.

AI Engine Application: Automatically schedules submergence-tolerant rice varieties (BRRI dhan51, 52) and short-cycle shifts in flood-prone char unions.

BARI & BWMRI (2025)

3. Terminal Heatwave & Canopy Temperature in Rabi Crops

Research Finding: BARI & BWMRI research shows heatwaves exceeding 35°C in February–March induce spikelet sterility, cutting wheat yields by 30–45%.

AI Engine Application: Monitors NASA MODIS LST to recommend early-maturing BARI Gom-33 or heat-resilient maize rotations.

FAO & IRRI (2022)

4. NASA SMAP L-Band & AWD Irrigation Scheduling

Research Finding: FAO & IRRI field trials demonstrate Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) saves 30–35% irrigation water without yield penalty.

AI Engine Application: Triggers pump advisories only when SMAP L4 root-zone moisture drops below critical thresholds, preventing excessive irrigation.
